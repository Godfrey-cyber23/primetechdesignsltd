FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to reach out.

How much does a typical project cost?

Project costs vary based on complexity, features, and timeline. A standard business website starts at $X, while complex web apps and cloud infrastructure are quoted custom. We provide a detailed, transparent quote after our initial discovery call.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

A standard informational website takes 2-4 weeks. Full-stack web applications and custom systems usually take 6-12 weeks depending on the scope. We establish clear milestones during the planning phase.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Absolutely. We offer flexible monthly maintenance retainer plans to ensure your system is secure, updated, and running smoothly. We also monitor cloud infrastructure 24/7 for our managed IT clients.

Can you work with clients outside of Zambia?

Yes! While we are based in Lusaka, we work with international clients seamlessly. We use tools like Slack, Zoom, and Trello to ensure smooth communication across any time zone.
